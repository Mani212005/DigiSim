## Transistor polarity relabelling

CGHD `transistor.fet` has no polarity. 156 transistors were labelled by eye on one reference photo per drawing and propagated to the other photos by position matching. Resulting instances: NMOS 355, PMOS 183, UNCERTAIN 86 (uncertain ones are flagged, kept out of the labels and stored as ignore regions).

## Ignored regions

Unmapped CGHD classes and undecided transistors/terminals are not labels. Their boxes are stored as ignore regions (`data_real/yolo/ignore/`) so the evaluator can mask them instead of counting a correct detection as a false positive.

## Caveats

- The pilot is 4 drafters, so the real test set is one drafter plus 3 Roboflow images. Gate classes other than AND/OR are far below the plan; XNOR does not exist in CGHD at all. Fetching more drafters is a one-line change to `real_data/config.py` (`CGHD_PILOT_SPLITS`) and changes the frozen hash.
- `terminal` boxes become INPUT_PORT, OUTPUT_PORT, VDD or GND only when the neighbouring text label says so (explicit in/out/supply names; component designators like R1 or Q1 never count). Terminals without such a label are ignore regions, not labels.
- CGHD `vss` (a rail arrow) is mapped to VDD and `voltage.dc` to VDD; `gnd` to GND. Batteries, AC sources, BJTs (no polarity relabel yet), diodes, op-amps and the other CGHD classes are ignore regions.
- JFET symbols (gate arrow) share the CGHD `transistor.fet` class and are left UNCERTAIN rather than called NMOS.
- The Roboflow and Sampleimages gate labels come from the source (polygons converted to boxes) and our own box labels; their images have unverified provenance.
